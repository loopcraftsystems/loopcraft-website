Methodology / 02

Our Proprietary Delivery Methodology.

Every LoopCraft engagement runs through the same four phases. This is The LOOP Framework©.

01

L: Listen & Learn

We start with a 90-minute structured discovery session. Every manual process consuming more than two hours per week gets mapped. You receive an AI Maturity Scorecard across 12 dimensions, your top three automation opportunities ranked by ROI and feasibility, and a full tool stack compatibility audit.

02

O: Orchestrate & Optimise

We define a phased, prioritised implementation plan with clear milestones, resource requirements, and measurable outcomes. No vague transformation narratives — only executable steps.

03

O: Operate & Automate

We deploy iteratively using 2-week sprint cycles. Each sprint delivers a working component — not slides. Stakeholders see progress from week one.

04

P: Prove & Perpetuate

We tie 3 to 5 KPIs directly to business outcomes. Week one includes daily check-ins and same-day hotfixes. Weeks two through four include weekly KPI reviews. The system gets smarter over time. Retainer clients receive a monthly report and quarterly The System review.
